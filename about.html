About Me

I am a Software Engineering graduate with a BS (Hons) degree from Liverpool John Moores University (LJMU). My passion for building impactful tools and solving real-world problems through programming has guided me toward this field. Over the past two years, I have strengthened my expertise in software development through coursework covering programming, object-oriented systems, data structures and algorithms, databases, and computer systems. This dedication earned me gold medal distinctions during my final two years at LJMU.

Currently, I am working as a Software Engineering Intern at Isee Waves, where I am involved in developing security-oriented solutions for software applications. This experience has allowed me to deepen my understanding of application security, software architecture, and secure coding practices, while contributing to meaningful projects that focus on building safe and reliable digital systems.

Beyond academics, I have gained valuable industry experience through internships at GreenX Solutions, DigiWop, CapreSoft, and NexusAI, where I worked on real-world projects that enhanced my web development skills. These experiences allowed me to work with technologies such as HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Bootstrap, React.js, AJAX, JavaFX, WordPress, Laravel, PHP, MySQL, and Golang.

In addition to software development, I am passionate about research and innovation. As a member of the All-Pakistan Student Union (APSU) Research Wing, I explored emerging methodologies and contributed to AI-based projects. I also collaborated with the Centre of Excellence for Technology, Quantum, and Artificial Intelligence Pakistan (CETQAP) under the mentorship of Dr. Zuhair Ahmed, which significantly deepened my understanding of quantum computing and advanced technologies.

I am also proud to be a recipient of the Amazon AI and ML Nanodegree Scholarship, through which I gained expertise in machine learning algorithms, data modeling, and AI architectures using Python, MATLAB, Scikit-learn, NumPy, and Pandas.

Beyond academics and research, I actively participate in communities that promote growth and inclusion in tech. I have been part of the Computer Science Society, SDG’s Society, Google Developer Student Clubs, and Women in Tech. Additionally, I have served as a Campus Ambassador for GirlScript Summer of Code and led my university’s badminton team as a player and captain.

Driven by curiosity and purpose, I strive to harness technology to create meaningful, user-centric, and innovative applications. I look forward to connecting with professionals, contributing to impactful projects, and continuing to grow as a developer and researcher.
